# Privacy Policy for Daykit

**Last updated:** October 2026

Daykit is developed by Tiny Spark. This policy explains how the app
handles your data.

## Your data stays on your device

Daykit has no account, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Your notes, calendar
events, alarms, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot
see, collect, or access this content.

Backup/export files and shared content (text or images) are created locally and
only sent anywhere if you choose to share or export them yourself.

## Advertising

Daykit shows ads through Google AdMob, which lets us offer the app for free. To
serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address, ad interaction
data, device identifiers (including your advertising ID), and basic diagnostic
data. This data is handled under Google's own Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage your ad personalization settings at
https://adssettings.google.com, or limit ad tracking in your Android device
settings.

## Permissions

Daykit requests notification, alarm, and background-service permissions solely
to make reminders, alarms, and timers work reliably. It does not request access
to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## Children's privacy

Daykit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above.
